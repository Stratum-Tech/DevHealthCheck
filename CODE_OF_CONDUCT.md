# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to making participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of experience level, gender, disability, ethnicity, religion, or nationality.

## Our Standards

**Encouraged:**
- Constructive, technical feedback
- Welcoming contributors at all experience levels
- Focusing on what is best for the project

**Not tolerated:**
- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks
- Trolling or deliberately disruptive behaviour
- Publishing others' private information without consent

## Enforcement

Violations can be reported to **conduct@stratum-tech.com**. Reports will be reviewed promptly and handled with discretion. Maintainers reserve the right to remove, edit, or reject contributions that violate this code of conduct.
